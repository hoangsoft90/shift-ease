ShiftEase — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026 · Applies to the ShiftEase Android application (package com.shiftease.shiftease).

HAIBA Software ("we", "us") built ShiftEase as an offline-first shift calendar. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app stores, what (little) leaves your device, and what rights you have.

Summary (TL;DR)
  • Your schedule data never leaves your device — no account, no cloud, no upload.
  • Two services connect to the internet from the app: crash reports (Sentry) and ads (Google AdMob).
  • Neither service receives your schedule content (jobs, shifts, pay rules, imports).
  • Contact: haibasoftware@gmail.com

1. Data stored on your device (never uploaded)

All of the following live exclusively in an encrypted local database (SQLCipher; the key is generated on-device and held by the platform secure storage — Android Keystore / iOS Keychain):

  • Jobs and their time zones, shift patterns and schedules
  • Individual shifts, edits and overrides
  • Pay rules you enter (hourly rates, differentials, overtime) and resulting income estimates
  • Import history of rosters you paste or upload
  • App settings

We operate servers that receive none of this data. Backups and calendar exports (.ics) are files you create and keep — we never see them.

2. Data sent over the internet

2.1 Crash reporting (Sentry)

When the app unexpectedly fails, it sends a crash report to Sentry (sentry.io, operated by Functional Software, Inc.) so we can fix the failure. Crash reports contain:

  • The error message and stack trace (technical cause of the failure)
  • App version, OS version, device model

Not included: your schedule content, pay rules, names, email, device identifiers, or precise location. Personally Identifiable Information is disabled in our Sentry configuration. Sentry's own privacy policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/

2.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland/LLC). For this, Google may process the advertising identifier of your device and approximate IP-derived data to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent choices.

  • Where required by law (EEA/UK), a consent form is shown at first use; you may accept or reject personalized advertising.
  • You can change or withdraw consent via your device's Android settings (Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID) or by clearing the app's storage.
  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not require an account and never collect your name, email or phone number through the app.
  • We do not read, upload, or analyze your schedule or pay information.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not track your location.

Data in transit is encrypted (TLS). Data at rest is encrypted on device (SQLCipher). There is no account, so account-based deletion does not apply — uninstalling the app removes all data stored on the device.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: Settings → Data → "Delete all data", or simply uninstall the app.
  • Opt out of ad personalization: via the in-app consent form or Android Privacy → Ads settings.
  • Disable crash reports: not currently configurable in-app; contact us and we will document alternatives.
  • Questions / data requests: email haibasoftware@gmail.com — we answer within 30 days.

6. Children

ShiftEase is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app's data flows change (for example, a new analytics service), this policy will be updated before the change ships, and the effective date above will change.
